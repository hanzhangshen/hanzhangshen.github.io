The Dual Imaginaries of Inland Modernization and the Ghost of Socialism: An Allegorical Reading of Guizhou

Hanzhang Shen and Yi Ouyang

JULY 2026 · CONFERENCE PRESENTATION

2026 International Conference on Digital Infrastructure and Local High-Quality Development
Guiyang, China

Overview

Using Guiyang’s Huaguoyuan as a case, this paper examines how infrastructure carries inland China’s imaginaries of modernization pursued under constrained conditions. Drawing on the politics and poetics of infrastructure, it reveals the historical articulation among real-estate development, digital infrastructure, and the legacy of Third Front construction, and unpacks the modernization promises, developmental visions, and utopian tensions behind Huaguoyuan’s “magical” landscape.
