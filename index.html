沈含章 / Hanzhang Shen

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Sociology at Zhejiang University (2025–2029).

My research interests center on historical and social scientific approaches to modern China, spanning the anthropology of religion, historical sociology, STS, intellectual history, and social history.

By examining how boundaries are drawn between religion and science, particularly around popular practices, I hope to historicize and contextualize the formation of particular epistemologies and historiographies, and to inquire into their political consequences. In my research, I seek to attend not only to religion and science but also to the materiality of technology and infrastructure.

As a student with a strong interest in Walter Benjamin and critical theory, I am also interested in the political aesthetics and cultural forms of modern China. These concerns extend to political economy, especially where it intersects with imaginaries of the future.

This website brings together my research, writing, and notes. It is also a space to keep track of questions and ideas as they develop.
